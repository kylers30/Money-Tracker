MY MONEY — EDITABLE FINANCE TRACKER

This version is designed to match the soft pastel planner-style dashboard you requested.

WHAT IS EDITABLE
- App name and currency symbol
- Wallet names and starting balances
- Transactions
- Expense/income categories
- Payment methods
- Monthly budgets
- Savings goals
- Bills and recurring payments

PRIVACY
Your finance data is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use.
Use Export Backup in Settings to save a JSON backup.

GITHUB PAGES UPDATE
Replace the old index.html, manifest.json, service-worker.js, and icons folder
with the files from this package, then commit your changes.


RECURRING BILLS
- Add a bill and choose Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, or Yearly.
- Choose its category, payment method, and wallet.
- Click Mark paid to record the bill as an expense.
- Recurring bills automatically move to the next due date.
- One-time bills are removed from upcoming bills after being marked paid.
